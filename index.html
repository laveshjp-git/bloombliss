The brewing ritual

Three steps to a slower minute

Each crystaltea is a handmade ball of isomalt with a signature tea blend sealed inside. You only need a cup and a kettle.

  1. STEP 1

    Heat the water

    Prepare 250–300 ml of hot water at 90–100°C.

  2. STEP 2

    Place the crystal

    Set one Bloombliss crystaltea in your cup, then pour the water over it.

  3. STEP 3

    Watch it bloom

    Give it 3–5 minutes while the shell melts and the flowers open. Then enjoy the moment.

    What the shell is made of

    A crystal you can drink

    The clear shell is isomalt, a low-calorie sweetener made from beet sugar and approved by the FDA and EFSA. It melts into the tea and adds a light sweetness without overpowering the leaves and flowers.
